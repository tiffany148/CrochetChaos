Every number in this chapter is arithmetic, generated by computer from the formulas shown. They save you a calculator at the yarn shop or the couch. None of them replaces a swatch: gauge always comes from your own blocked sample.

> [!STATUS]
> Tables are calculated, not measured. Stitch counts are rounded to the nearest whole stitch; round again to fit your stitch multiple (Chapter 39).
