These sources were consulted for the subjects identified in each entry. Original examples and exercises are written for this book. Reference consultation does not establish physical testing or permission to reproduce external artwork.

S01. Craft Yarn Council. Crochet Abbreviations Master List. Source link. Consulted for US abbreviations, US–UK equivalents, and the need for local special-stitch definitions. Applies primarily to Chapters 1, 4, 9–14, 16, 19. Basic names are factual terminology; avoid reproducing the complete publisher table as supplied artwork.

S02. Craft Yarn Council. Crochet Chart Symbols. Source link. Consulted for chart-key precedence and stitch-symbol concepts. Chapters 12 and 14. Official symbol artwork is not used. Permission and attribution requirements must be checked before artwork is added.

S03. Craft Yarn Council. Standard Yarn Weight System. Source link. Consulted for category names and the guideline status of sizes and gauges. Chapter 5 contains a short text cross-reference, not official category-symbol artwork or the full gauge/hook chart. Publication permission/notification requirements remain to be resolved for any later use of official standards material.

S04. Craft Yarn Council. FAQs. Source link. Consulted for the warning that weight categories do not establish yarn interchangeability. Chapters 6–7.

S05. Craft Yarn Council. Hooks and Needles. Source link. Consulted for tool-sizing conventions. Chapter 2.

S06. Craft Yarn Council. Yarn Label Information. Source link. Consulted for label fields and care-symbol context. Chapters 5–8.

S07. Leinhauser, Jean. How to Read a Crochet Pattern. Craft Yarn Council. Source link. Consulted for pattern vocabulary, grouping, and repeats. Chapters 13 and 16. Original examples in this book use their own explicitly stated punctuation conventions.

S08. Craft Yarn Council. Schematics. Source link. Consulted for dimensional drawings and comparison with a well- fitting garment. Chapters 15, 18, 21, 23.

S09. Craft Yarn Council. Standard Body Measurements and Sizing. Source link. Consulted for body-versus-garment measurements and ease terminology. Chapters 15 and 18. Official body-size tables are not reproduced. Later grading must use documented sizing assumptions, not invented measurements.

S10. Crochet Guild of America. Learn to Crochet. Source link. Consulted for beginner terminology and availability of handed teaching. Chapters 1 and 9. No instructional images are reproduced.

S11. Lion Brand Yarn. Glossary Single Crochet. Source link. Consulted for basic stitch mechanics. Chapter 9 and short dictionary cross-checks in Chapter 18. The rectangle, diagnostics, and pedagogical sequence are original.

S12. Lion Brand Yarn. Glossary Half Double Crochet. Source link. Consulted for the three-loop closure. Chapter 18.

S13. Lion Brand Yarn. Glossary Double Crochet. Source link. Consulted for the two successive two-loop reductions and a counted-chain convention. Chapter 18. This book's exercises state their own chain conventions and do not assume that this source's convention is universal.

S14. Yarnspirations. Basics of Crochet for Beginners. Source link. Consulted for basic movements, grips, stitch entries, and overview terminology. Chapters 2–4, 9, 12. The current page includes an apparent text-editing error in its introductory double-crochet paragraph; the book follows the explicit mechanics cross-checked with S13 rather than copying that sentence.

S15. Lion Brand Yarn. How to Crochet Gauge. Source link. Consulted for hook adjustment direction and gauge importance. Chapters 7 and 17.

S16. Crochet.com. All About Crochet Gauge. Source link. Consulted for measurement spans, larger samples, and treatment conditions. Chapter 16.

S17. Lion Brand Yarn. How Do I Substitute One Yarn for Another. Source link. Consulted for gauge and fabric acceptability. Chapters 7 and 20. No fixed universal growth or reserve percentage is adopted.

S18. Woolmark. Wool Fibre. Source link. Consulted for broad wool recovery properties. Chapter 3. Marketing claims are not used as comparative environmental rankings or guarantees for all wool yarns.

S19. Woolmark. Caring for Wool. Source link. Consulted for care-claim precedence. Chapters 5 and 8. No claim is made that all wool tolerates machine washing or drying.

S20. Cotton Incorporated. Fibers booklet. Source link. Consulted for cotton absorbency and general textile properties. Chapter 3. Finished-yarn behavior is treated as sample-dependent.

S21. Lion Brand Yarn. Yarn Label and Care. Source link. Consulted for label-led care. Chapter 65.

S22. Lion Brand Yarn Support. Chenille loose loops or worming. Source link. Consulted only for worming terminology and construction dependence. Chapter 5. The page's suggested knitting remedies are not transposed into verified crochet remedies.

S23. Eckman, Edie. Extended Crochet Stitches. November 12, 2023. Source link. Consulted for the added one-loop draw- through concept and extended single crochet. Chapter 18. Its written extended-double entry currently repeats 'yarn over' at the beginning; that conflicts with its own general description and the resulting loop accounting. The book's one-wrap extended-double sequence is independently cross-checked against S25. Retain this discrepancy in the technical review record.

S24. Lion Brand Yarn. Unlock the Secrets of Crochet Cables. Source link. Consulted for front/back post insertion paths. Chapter 19. No cable pattern is reproduced.

S25. Scheepjes. Universe in Bloom Tips and Tricks US version 3. Source link. Consulted selectively for mechanical distinctions among shared-closure stitches, popcorns, puffs, and the one-wrap extended double crochet. Chapters 9 and 11. Its pattern, photographs, charts, motifs, and named design are not reproduced. This book defines its own puff variant with three draw-up actions, not this reference's four-action variant.

S26. Lion Brand Yarn. How to Crochet Invisible Sewn Seam. Source link. Consulted for alternating row-end attachment. Chapter 62.

S27. Lion Brand Yarn. How to Crochet Slip Stitch Seam. Source link. Consulted for hook-worked seaming. Chapter 62.

S28. Lion Brand Yarn. How to Crochet Single Crochet Seam. Source link. Consulted for the single crochet seam structure. Chapter 62.

S29. Craft Yarn Council. Seaming Crochet. Source link. Consulted for mattress-style seam tension and avoiding shortening. Chapter 62.

S30. Permann, Linda. Crochet Buttonholes and Button Bands. Creative Crochet Corner. Source link. Consulted for chain- and-skip openings, working into the next-row chains, and testing button size. Chapter 26. No project instructions are reproduced.

S31. IngramSpark. Print Book File Guidelines. Source link. Consulted for single-page interiors, separate cover files, embedded fonts, and minimum safe margins. Production only. The current PDF is a reading proof, not a final PDF/X- compliant publication interior. Final trim, binding, paper, color mode, page count constraints, font embedding, and current platform requirements must be checked again after text and illustration completion.

S32. Tin Can Knits. How to Choose Your Size. Source link. Consulted for preferred-garment comparisons in Chapter 37. Knitting construction instructions are not substituted for crochet. No source pattern is reproduced.

S33. Cocoknits. How to Measure Garments to Get Preferred Pattern Size. Source link. Consulted for comparing actual garment dimensions and fit preferences. Chapters 25 and 28. Examples are original and hypothetical.

S34. Dora Does. Six Common Ways to Construct a Crochet Sweater. Source link. Consulted for construction-family distinctions in Chapter 40. No source garment pattern is reproduced.

S35. Dora Does. How to Construct a Simple Drop Shoulder Crochet Sweater. Source link. Consulted for panel and seam relationships. Chapter 40's counts, neckline, and sleeve schedule are original.

S36. Dora Does. How to Adjust Top-Down Yoke Crochet Sweaters to Fit. Source link. Consulted for separating circumference and depth decisions. Chapters 26 and 28.

S37. PDSA. Does My Dog Need to Wear a Coat on Winter Walks. Source link. Consulted for individual need, basic coat measurements, and movement. Chapters 29–30. No temperature or medical-treatment thresholds are prescribed in this book.

S38. PDSA. Is It OK to Dress Up My Pet. Source link. Consulted for appropriate purpose and comfort concerns. Chapter 47. Garments are not presented as prescribed medical or anxiety treatments.

S39. RSPCA Australia. Animal Welfare Issues with Pets Wearing Costumes. Source link. Consulted for supervision, behavioral interference, head enclosure, and unsafe attachments. Chapters 29 and 31. Hoodie detail is not recommended as enclosing canine headwear.

S40. Ruffwear. Understanding Layering Systems. Source link. Consulted for manufacturer-specific examples of over/under harness layering and portals. Chapter 50. This is not manufacturer approval of the original crochet samples.

S41. PlanetJune. How to Crochet a Magic Ring. Source link. Consulted for basic adjustable-loop structure. Chapter 51. The assisted-strand test is an original unverified experiment; no source image or project is reproduced.

S42. PlanetJune. Invisible Decrease. Source link. Consulted for distinguishing ordinary and invisible decreases. Chapters 9 and 34. The original rounded-form pattern explicitly uses ordinary sc2tog.

S43. PlanetJune. Joining Amigurumi. Source link. Consulted for open-rim-to-surface joining concepts. Chapter 53. No external plush design is reproduced.

S44. US Consumer Product Safety Commission. Small Parts Ban and Choking Hazard Labeling. Source link. Consulted for small-part context. Chapter 53 does not claim that embroidered features or a home-made object establish product compliance. No exhaustive regulatory summary is attempted; current applicable requirements must be consulted for a real children's product.

S45. Yarnspirations. Guide to Granny Crochet. Source link. Consulted for traditional group and corner concepts. Chapter 27 uses an independently written traditional-construction exercise with fresh joins and explicit chain spaces; no source illustration or complete source project is reproduced.

S46. Crochet.com. Filet Crochet. Source link. Consulted for open/filled blocks and chart proportions. Chapter 56 uses an original diamond chart and explicitly defined boundary-first method rather than copying the source chart or market-bag pattern.

S47. Parker, Ashley, for Clover. How to Do the Tunisian Simple Stitch. Source link. Consulted for pickup, return, and edge structure. Chapter 57 uses an original 12-column sample. Source text begins with 20 chains and later mentions 10 loops; this inconsistency is not reproduced. Physical sample verification remains required.

S48. Crochet.com. Broomstick Lace. Source link. Consulted for retained long loops and five-loop grouping. Chapter 57 contains an independently written one-band study; repeating-fabric edge mechanics need additional development and physical verification.

S49. Clover. Hair Pin Lace Tool. Source link. Consulted only for tool structure and strip concept, not its historical origin assertions. Chapter 57's hairpin coverage is currently introductory; a full checked setup, strip, and joining lesson remains a depth-pass requirement.

S50. Lowman, Susan. Big Announcement My New Annie's Video. The Crochet Architect. Source link. Consulted for specialist repair-teaching context and use of matching mistake samples. No paid class instructions are accessed or reproduced. Chapters 42–44's original protocols do not imply verified internal cutting repairs.

S51. Crochet.com. How to Block Crochet. Source link. Consulted for broad finishing distinctions. Chapter 63. Fiber- specific care remains label-led; no universal steam treatment is adopted.

S52. Craft Yarn Council. Designer Pitfalls. Source link. Consulted for complete instructions, schematic, and sizing responsibilities. Chapters 45–47. The basket and grading tables are original hypothetical constructions, not source patterns.

S53. Craft Yarn Council. Industry Standards and Guidelines for Designers. Source link. Consulted for pattern-production responsibilities. Chapter 73. Consultation is not evidence that the book has received an independent technical edit.
